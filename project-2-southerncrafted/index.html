Southern Crafted - Home goods website

Student Team Project

Year

2024

Duration

10 weeks

Overview

Redesigning Southern Crafted’s e-commerce experience to make local craftsmanship easier to discover, trust, and purchase online.

My Roles

UX Designer: I focused on restructuring the e-commerce experience, translating research insights into clearer information architecture and high-fidelity interface design. I also helped shape the design system and validate key flows through usability testing.

Design Outcomes

89%

Nearly all tested users preferred the redesigned experience for browsing and purchasing products.

32%

In average task completion rate.

(product purchasing process for customer)

8 Awards

From Indigo Design Awards (Including Gold in Websites & Digital Design, etc) and ENTELECHY 2025 Best Web UI

📍 Context

Why Southern Crafted?

Southern Crafted is a Savannah-based retailer selling American South handcrafted home goods.

The physical store had warmth and low country luxury character, but the original website did not clearly communicate the brand spirit or why the products were worth trusting.

Original Home Page

Why is original website need to redesign?

1. The homepage gave the wrong first impression and felt closer to a restaurant than a home goods store.

2. Product categories and navigation were scattered, making shopping feel harder than it needed to be.

3. The connection between products and craftsman was weak, which reduced trust and emotional value.

🔍 Research

Understanding How People Decide to Buy Handmade Goods Online

We went to the physical store to observe and interview some customers.

Observation

Observe customers who bought the product in store

16 Interviews

Interview with customers who bought the product in store and online customers

🎯 Problem

Finding our focus

We translated our research findings into a focused question to guide the design.

HMW help users to easily navigate, find relevant information,
and better engage with the southern crafted’s identity?

💡 Design Strategy

Turning insights into action

Building on the problem statement, we defined three design priorities to guide decisions across the shopping experience.

01

Simplify the shopping path

Reorganize the information architecture of home page and product details page.

02

Bring craftspeople into the purchase journey

Connect the craftsman with the products, materials and the production process.

03

Make handmade products easier to trust

Enhance product details, reviews, stock, shipping and care information.

✅ Final Prototype

Visual Refresh

With a completely new design system, Southern Crafted's website began to align with a luxury but warm high-end home goods store.
